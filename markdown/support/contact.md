# Support

As a certified Shopgate Partner you have access to a number of support options.

## Slack

During your onboarding process you should have been invited to the Slack workspace [Shopgate Partners](https://shopgate-partners.slack.com/).

Use the public channel #general or private channels for your organization to discuss issues around specific projects you are working on.

## Tickets

Ticket-based support is available through our [Support portal](https://support.shopgate.com/).

## E-Mail

You can ask general questions using our support email address: [developer-support@shopgate.com](mailto:developer-support@shopgate.com)
